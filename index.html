About The Institute

A Next-Generation Gurukul for India’s Future Officers

Welcome to Dehradun Defence College, operating in synergy with Doon Public Senior Secondary School (under the patronage of Director Mr. Zaheer Ahmed) and guided by Director Mr. Gaurav Sharma and College Enrollment Director Ms. Neha Kukreja. Located in the serene foothills of Maldevta Valley, our institution bridges the gap between top board schooling and elite defence officer development.

We don’t believe in rote memorization. Our cadets live a structured defence routine starting with morning physical drills, followed by concept-driven UPSC lectures in smart classrooms, afternoon group discussions, and evening SSB obstacle course practice.
